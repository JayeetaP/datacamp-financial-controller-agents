# Business specification

The controller needs to decide whether the fictional September 2026 management-reporting package
may advance to human review or whether a material item must be reviewed, escalated, or blocked.

The system should shorten evidence collection while preserving deterministic admission checks,
calculation ownership, source lineage, and human approval. Success means a reviewer can see what
happened, what proves it, what code calculated, what remains unresolved, and what action is needed.

The system cannot post or approve accounting entries and cannot treat historical precedent as
current-period proof.
